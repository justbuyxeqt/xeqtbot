# Should I just buy XEQT in my FHSA?

The typical advice for XEQT is to buy it when you have a long time horizon and can afford to weather the volatility. In that sense, it's not wise to take too much risk with money you might need on the short to medium term, i.e. when a large sum of money will be needed in the coming years to buy a house.

That said, there is no rule that says the specific money that comes from an FHSA withdrawal has to be used on a house. For the FHSA withdrawal to be tax-free, it mainly just needs to happen around the same time as the home purchase. Money is fungible, and the FHSA is an awesome tax shelter – depositing $8,000 in an FHSA gets you a tax deduction regardless of what you buy inside that FHSA with the money.

With that in mind, the real question you need to ask yourself is: "*How screwed am I if XEQT crashes right when I need the money to buy a home?*"

* If your home-purchasing timeline is strict and you plan to heavy rely on your FHSA savings, you probably don't want to risk these savings.

* If your plans are flexible and you're comfortable with the idea that a big crash might force you to wait a few more years to buy a home, you might afford to take more risk with your savings.

* If you're not even sure you want to buy a home at all, you're always better off prioritizing the FHSA over the RRSP. You still get the tax deduction, but you keep the possibility of an qualified tax-free withdrawal if you do decide to buy a home, and in the worst case you convert your FHSA into RRSP after 15 years, effectively meaning you got extra RRSP room for free. In the meantime your actual RRSP room keeps accumulating every year.

* If you have maxed out your FHSA (and other relevant registered accounts) and still have enough savings that could be used on the house regardless of any possible XEQT crash, then you're better off using your FHSA to host XEQT while you keep the low-yielding safe savings outside the FHSA. Once you buy a home, you can liquidate the XEQT and redirect the proceeds to another account to buy the same amount of XEQT in there and keep your overall exposure the same.
