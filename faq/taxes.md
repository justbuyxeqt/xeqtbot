#### How are my XEQT gains taxed?

In a registered account, the only things that matter for taxes are the deposits and withdrawals. Gains on your XEQT are not taxed in your TFSA, for example. You can buy and sell whenever you want, quarterly distributions drop as cash in the TFSA have no impact on your taxes or contribution room (reinvest them automatically)

In a taxable account you have to track your ACB to figure out how much capital gains (or losses) you realize when you sell. Each buy increases your ACB, then later on each sale, distribution will create a taxable event that may affect your taxes when you file for that year.
